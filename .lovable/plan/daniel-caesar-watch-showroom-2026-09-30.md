# Daniel Caesar Watch Showroom

## Goal
Build the first release of Daniel Caesar’s bilingual luxury watch website: an immersive showroom inspired by Bellano’s product scale, but with its own navy, dark-grey, and ivory editorial identity.

## Experience
- Use the selected Mist & Ivory composition as the structural reference: refined header, statement watch feature, technical highlights, after-care verification, atelier story, and restrained footer.
- Keep the chosen Sora/Manrope typography and navy/dark-grey/ivory palette; use metallic accents sparingly rather than making gold dominant.
- Create responsive desktop and mobile layouts with subtle, precise reveals and image movement.
- Generate cohesive, original watch and craftsmanship imagery for Daniel Caesar rather than copying Bellano assets.

## Pages and navigation
- **Home:** featured collection, brand statement, technical highlights, category previews, journal stories, and warranty entry point.
- **Watches:** browsable showroom with filters for style and features.
- **Watch detail:** large gallery, specifications, materials, movement, water resistance, and warranty summary.
- **Journal:** editorial stories about design, craftsmanship, and care.
- **Warranty:** serial-number lookup interface with clear verification states.
- **Brand:** story, philosophy, and craftsmanship.
- Use separate, shareable pages with unique titles and descriptions.

## Languages
- Add English and Persian controls only.
- Switch the full interface between left-to-right English and right-to-left Persian, including navigation and product information.
- Use polished starter copy in both languages, aligned with the roadmap’s international and Iranian brand roles.

## Showroom content
- Organize watches by style: Dress, Sport, Chronograph, and Skeleton.
- Organize by features such as automatic movement, power reserve, case material, water resistance, and sapphire crystal.
- Supply an initial curated set of fictional Daniel Caesar collections, models, specifications, and journal entries so the experience is complete; these remain replaceable when final catalog data is provided.

## Warranty scope
- Build the complete customer-facing lookup flow and its success, invalid, and not-found states.
- Use clearly identified demonstration records for this first release; live warranty activation, customer records, QR issuance, SMS, and service history remain a later Cloud-backed phase from the roadmap.

## Technical details
- Keep the existing TanStack application structure and add shared navigation, language state, product data, and reusable showroom elements.
- Define all visual values as semantic design tokens and load fonts through the document head.
- Add accessible controls, keyboard-visible focus, reduced-motion behavior, and route-specific social metadata.
- Validate the final experience at desktop and mobile sizes, including filters, language direction, product navigation, and warranty states.
