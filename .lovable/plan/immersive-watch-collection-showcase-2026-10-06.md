# Immersive Watch & Collection Showcase

## What will be added
- Add a full-width **Signature Timepieces** section to the home page, inspired by the reference video but styled for Daniel Caesar.
- Let visitors switch between collections and immediately see the selected watch, its story, price, reference, and core specifications.
- Add a **Closer Look** presentation with large watch imagery and selectable detail points for the dial, case, movement, crystal, and water resistance.
- Add a cinematic **Precision Within** band using movement imagery, compact technical facts, and a clear path to the selected watch’s full page.
- Keep every label, description, and control bilingual in English and Persian, with correct RTL behavior.

## Visual direction
- Preserve the existing navy, dark-grey, and ivory palette, with restrained metallic highlights.
- Use original Daniel Caesar watch imagery rather than copying Bellano assets.
- Match the video’s editorial rhythm: dark full-width scenes, oversized product views, concise typography, and subtle transitions.
- Ensure the interaction works as a stable selector on desktop and a swipe-friendly layout on mobile.

## Product detail structure
- Extend the shared showroom catalog with translated detail stories and feature callouts for every watch.
- Reuse that shared content on the home showcase and individual watch pages so specifications stay synchronized.
- Keep the experience inquiry-led; no cart or checkout will be introduced.
